# Memo: Where to focus provincial procurement audits in 2026

**To:** Provincial inspectorate (Inspektorat) or an internal-audit / forensic team (illustrative)
**Data:** SPSE public tender pages of all 34 provincial governments, 2025 tender lists. 4,165 completed tenders, of which 4,145 have full bid details. Scraped 30 Sep to 1 Oct 2026.

## Bottom line

Start with **single-bidder tenders in a handful of provinces and public-works offices**. That is where competition is weakest and where the price difference shows.

- 28% of tenders (1,155) carry two or more red flags. They are worth Rp 2,817 billion, or 14% of the contract value analysed.
- Without competition, winners priced almost at the owner's estimate. The median single bid was about 1% below HPS, against 5–7% in competitive tenders. Had single-bidder tenders matched that discount, they would have cost about Rp 266 billion less (an indication, not a measured loss).
- A shortlist of 209 tenders, each with a stated reason, is ready for review in the Power BI report.

## Evidence

**1. Red flags across all 4,145 tenders**

| Red flag | Share of tenders |
|---|---|
| Single bidder | 31.3% |
| Cheapest bid did not win (lowest-price tenders) | 27.3% |
| Won at 99% or more of the owner's estimate | 23.4% |
| Repeat winner (3+ wins in the same work unit) | 18.1% |
| Possible package split | 7.0% |
| Bids within 1% of each other | 2.9% |

**2. Price without competition** (median winning bid below the owner's estimate)

| Family | With competition | Single bidder | Single-bidder tenders |
|---|---|---|---|
| Works and goods tenders | 7.2% | 1.1% | 513 |
| Consultant selections | 5.4% | 1.0% | 783 |

**3. Where the risk sits.** Share of tenders with 2+ red flags, for provinces with at least 20 analysed tenders:

| Province | Tenders | 2+ flags | Single bidder | Won at 99%+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sumatera Selatan | 349 | 80% | 82% | 77% |
| Kalimantan Tengah | 211 | 60% | 70% | 51% |
| Jambi | 73 | 48% | 51% | 42% |
| Lampung | 239 | 47% | 81% | 41% |
| Sulawesi Tengah | 42 | 38% | 14% | 52% |

At the other end, Nusa Tenggara Timur (0%), Sulawesi Selatan (1.4%), Nusa Tenggara Barat (1.9%) and Aceh (2.5%) show few multi-flag tenders.

The flagged value is concentrated in a few work units:
- Dinas PU Bina Marga dan Tata Ruang, Sumatera Selatan: Rp 620.6 billion.
- Dinas Sumber Daya Air, DKI Jakarta: Rp 565.0 billion.
- Dinas Pekerjaan Umum dan Penataan Ruang, Kalimantan Tengah: Rp 350.6 billion.

**4. Consultant selections are riskier.** Single bidder 36% vs 26% for works tenders. Two or more flags 34% vs 21%.

**5. A system-wide weakness.** In 69% of tenders the owner's estimate (HPS) equals the budget ceiling (pagu). The estimate seems to copy the budget instead of coming from a market price survey, which removes a key price check for every tender.

## Recommendations

1. **Review the 209-tender shortlist first, starting with Sumatera Selatan, Kalimantan Tengah and Lampung.** Each tender comes with its reason, such as "Same firm keeps winning in this work unit (11 wins vs typical 2)". That turns a 4,000-tender population into a few weeks of focused work.
2. **Ask why single-bidder tenders had one bidder.** Check how widely the tender was announced, the qualification requirements, and how many firms registered but did not bid. Shortlisted tenders received a median of 2 bids, against 3 across all tenders.
3. **Look at repeat winners together with package splitting.** 189 company and work-unit pairs won 3 or more times in 2025, and 289 tenders show a possible split. Review these by work unit, not tender by tender.
4. **Fix the estimate process system-wide.** Require a documented price survey for HPS, at least above a value threshold, so that HPS stops copying pagu.
5. **Keep the monitor running.** The pipeline refreshes weekly and stops itself if the data-quality checks fail, so the shortlist stays current as more 2025 tenders complete.

## Caveats

- **Flags are screening signals, not findings.** One flag is common and often innocent. For example, the cheapest bidder is often disqualified for a valid reason.
- **Price comparison:** the Rp 266 billion is indicative. Single-bidder tenders may be smaller, more remote or more technical than competitive ones.
- **Coverage:** 20 completed tenders were excluded because their winner page on SPSE is empty. Direct procurement and e-katalog purchases are outside the scope.
- **Small provinces:** shares from fewer than 20 tenders are not compared.
- **Independence:** this is independent analysis of public data and is not affiliated with LKPP or any government body.
